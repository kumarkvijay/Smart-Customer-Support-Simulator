# TechGear Phone Warranty Guide

TechGear phones include a one-year limited warranty that covers manufacturing defects under normal consumer use. The warranty does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage, unauthorized repair attempts, or cosmetic wear.

If battery health drops below 80 percent within the first 12 months and diagnostics confirm normal charging behavior, support can review the device for battery service.

Proof of purchase is required for warranty approval. If the device shows signs of impact damage, support should escalate the case for manual review rather than promising a free replacement.
