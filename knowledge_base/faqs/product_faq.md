# Product FAQ

## Laptop screen flicker
If a customer reports screen flicker, ask them to lower the refresh rate to 60 Hz, update the display driver, disable adaptive brightness, and test with an external monitor. If the flicker continues after a driver reinstall and external monitor test, escalate for hardware review.

## Laptop overheating
Advise the customer to place the laptop on a hard surface, clear dust from vents, update the BIOS if available, and reduce high-performance graphics settings before scheduling a repair.

## Phone battery drain
Ask the customer to check battery health, disable background refresh for unused apps, and install the latest system update. If battery capacity falls below 80 percent within the warranty period, support can review a replacement request.

## Docking station issues
If a monitor or accessory is not detected through the dock, reseat the USB-C cable, update the dock firmware, and test a direct device connection.
